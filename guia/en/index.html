In brief

The safe path is a free INEMA guide to help you and your family get through the coming years of instability: floods, tornadoes, and heat during a very strong El Niño; blackouts, attacks on systems and data leaks; and work changing with robots and artificial intelligence. There are 5 simple steps: build a home kit, avoid relying only on online access, protect your accounts, take care of your health and make a family plan, and prepare your work for AI. You don’t need anything to read it. If you want, a small program can put together a plan for your household in one minute.

Note: the plan program and the checklists in the repository are in Portuguese, and some details (emergency numbers 199/193/192, SMS 40199, Registrato, CPF, Pix) apply to Brazil. Outside Brazil, use your country's emergency numbers and civil protection guidance; the amounts (water, food, 72 hours) are the same.

Why now

When one system stops, everything stops

In this week alone, November 2026, three warnings that the world will stop more often — and prepared families suffer less.

✈️ Flights stopped in the US

On November 13, 2026, the FAA suspended departures at all airports in the United States because of an air traffic control communications failure (not an attack). Thousands of flights were delayed or canceled. If a failure can stop the world’s wealthiest country, it can happen in your city too. source

💻 Attacks in Brazil

The Port of Santos had its systems knocked offline in an attack (since November 6). Caixa Vida e Previdência exposed data from about 7.6 million customers. The National Treasury exposed 30,000 records and 4,000 Pix keys. The danger now is a scam using your correct name and CPF. Sources: Port · Caixa Vida · Treasury

🌧️ Strong to very strong El Niño

El Niño (a warmer Pacific Ocean that changes rainfall in Brazil) is already underway (declared in June). In its November 12, 2026 bulletin, NOAA, the US weather agency, says it keeps strengthening and that a strong-to-very strong El Niño is likely through March 2027 (more than a 79% chance; in August and September, the chance of it becoming very strong was above 90%) (NOAA bulletin). In Brazil: more rain, storms, and flooding in the South; heat, drought, and fires in the Center-North. Greater risk doesn’t mean disaster is certain, but it’s time to prepare.

🧭 A perspective from someone who monitors the climate

"I’ve worked with technology for 40 years, monitor the climate, have been to Antarctica and the Arctic, and have worked with companies, education, and government. My view is that five very turbulent years lie ahead, almost like a war: climate, attacks, power struggles, factions, and the economy changing with robots and AI. And today I can say: we can get through this difficult period." — Nei Maldaner, INEMA

The path

The 5 steps of the safe path

Do them in order. Each step fits into a weekend. The details and checklists are in the kit (how to use it ↓).

1 · Home→2 · Offline→3 · Digital→4 · Health and people→5 · Work

🏠 1. Home: a 72-hour kit, then 14 days

This is what Civil Defense (Brazil) recommends (and the European Union has too, since 2025): 2 liters of water per person per day, nonperishable food, medicine, a flashlight and battery-powered radio, a portable charger, documents in a waterproof bag, and contacts on paper. A family of 4 needs 24 liters of water for 3 days. Then gradually build up to 14 days and review every 6 months.

📴 2. Don’t rely only on online access

If Pix, your banking app, or the internet stops working, how will you buy food? Keep enough cash for a few days of expenses (don’t withdraw your bank savings), contacts, prescriptions, and documents on paper, a map and information saved offline, and 14 days of food already at home. Library without internet: 3 options ↓

🔐 3. Digital protection

Your data may already have been leaked. Be wary of anyone who knows your name and CPF and asks for personal information, a password, or Pix. Turn on two-step verification (an extra code in addition to your password): for email, use an authenticator app (such as Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator) instead of SMS; for WhatsApp, use “two-step verification” with a 6-digit PIN; for your bank, use the token in its own app. Use a different password for each account. Also check Registrato (a free Central Bank website) for accounts and Pix keys opened under your CPF.

❤️ 4. Health and people

Keep a 14-day supply of prescription medicine and store the prescription. Make a family plan: a meeting place, who picks up the children, a relative outside town everyone can contact, and how to reach each other without internet. Trusted neighbors. And Civil Defense (Brazil) alerts on your phone: text your ZIP code to 40199.

🗺️ Different regions, different risks

The kit includes checklists for floods and landslides, severe winds and tornadoes, heat and fires, long blackouts, digital scams, and supply shortages. Choose the ones for your city, and the plan will include only what matters to you.

Step 5

Get through to the other side with a job

Robots and artificial intelligence will take over a large part of the work. Companies that don’t prepare will have to change or fail. Those who prepare will change roles; those who wait will be laid off.

📏 Find out your AI level

The kit’s free nivel-ia certificate shows how much of your work AI already does, on a 5-step scale: from AL1 (you do it alone) to AL5 (AI does it alone, with rules and alerts). If you only use ChatGPT in a browser, use the paper form. It’s a measurement, not a diploma.

📈 Evidence from someone who measured

Nei measured his own work over 90 days: on average over the period, 48.9% was already handled by AI; week by week, it rose from 11% (late August) to 61% (the last week). This is a measurement of his machine, not a national statistic — but it shows how quickly things are changing.

Technical section

The kit: for anyone who wants a ready-made household plan

The technical section starts here: download the kit and run a simple program that puts together your plan. You don’t need this section to understand and follow what’s above.

What it is

A plan for your home in one minute

The kit is a folder with the 5 steps in checklist form and a Python program that does the calculations for you. It works offline: nothing leaves your computer.

🧮 The right calculations

Water (2 L per person per day) and food (2,000 kcal per person per day) for 72 hours and 14 days, in 5 L jugs and kilograms of dry food.

🗺️ Only your risks

Choose floods, severe winds, heat, blackouts, digital risks, or conflict; the plan includes the checklist for each. Children, older adults, pets, and medicine are included if you say they apply.

📌 Fridge sheet

Civil Defense (Brazil) 199, Fire Department 193, SAMU 192, meeting place and who picks up the children, plus the date of the next review (6 months).

Requirements

What you need

Only Python 3.9 or newer (already included on Linux and Mac; on Windows, install it from python.org and use py instead of python3 in the commands). No extra libraries.

Check Python

Open a terminal and run:

python3 --version  # Python 3.9 ou mais

No terminal?

Use the calculator on this page and follow the checklists in the passos/ folder on GitHub by hand.

User guide

Step by step

From scratch to a plan posted on the fridge.

1

Download the kit

Using git, or the “Code → Download ZIP” button on GitHub.

git clone https://github.com/inematds/caminho-seguro
cd caminho-seguro
2

Answer the questions

How many people, children, older adults, pets, whether anyone takes regular medication, and the risks in your region. Press Enter to accept the default.

python3 caminho.py  # grava meu-plano.md
3

Or enter everything at once

Possible risks: enchente, vendaval, calor, apagao, digital, conflito.

python3 caminho.py --nome "Família Silva" --pessoas 4 --criancas 1 --remedios --riscos enchente,apagao,digital --saida meu-plano.md
# ok: meu-plano.md — 72 h: 24 L de água; revise em 6 meses
python3 caminho.py --listar-riscos
4

Print and check off

Open meu-plano.md (in any text editor), print it, put the fridge sheet on the fridge, and check off the items as you go. Details for each step are in passos/1-casa-72h.md to passos/5-trabalho-ia.md.

5

Review every 6 months

The plan includes the date. Run it again if your family changes. For anyone working with the code, the tests are:

python3 -m venv .venv && . .venv/bin/activate && pip install pytest  # só para os testes
python3 -m pytest -q tests  # 17 passed
